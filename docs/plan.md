# Subatone — Master Plan

> CRED for subscriptions and recurring payments. Track everything. Pay through us. Get rewarded.

---

## The Big Idea

CRED built a ₹6,000 Cr business by making credit card bill payments rewarding. Their insight: the act of paying a bill is a captive moment of high intent — wrap it in rewards, and you own the user's habit.

**Subatone applies the same principle to the subscriptions space.**

Every person paying for Netflix, Spotify, rent, ChatGPT, gym, and insurance every month is a captive, high-frequency, high-intent payer. There is no reward for doing it. No visibility. No control. No fun.

Subatone changes that.

---

## Problem Statement

Individuals today juggle dozens of recurring financial commitments spread across different apps, banks, and billing cycles — OTT platforms, mobile recharges, AI tools, gym memberships, SaaS tools, insurance premiums, rent, and more. There is no single place to see the full picture, which leads to:

- Forgotten subscriptions that keep charging silently
- Overspending on overlapping services
- No awareness of total monthly/annual burn
- Missed renewal dates causing service interruptions
- Zero rewards for being a consistent, on-time payer

---

## Solution

**Subatone** is a gamified subscription and recurring payment platform — part tracker, part payment router, part rewards engine. Users track every recurring deduction, pay through Subatone, and earn **Suba Coins** on every payment. Coins unlock cashbacks, brand deals, and exclusive perks.

## Gamification Principles (Reward / Risk Framework)

### Reward Loop
- Every on-time payment = Suba Coins
- Streaks multiply coins (more months unbroken = higher multiplier)
- Tier upgrades unlock better rewards, exclusive deals, higher coin rates
- Achievements and badges are shareable social proof

### Risk / Loss Aversion Loop
- Breaking a payment streak resets progress — users feel the cost of missing
- "Streak Shield" allows spending coins to protect a streak (drives coin redemption, drives payment)
- Tier demotion risk if activity drops — users maintain engagement to preserve status
- Free trial expiry alerts prevent accidental charges (builds trust, keeps users engaged)

### Why It Works
Behavioural economics principles embedded in every touchpoint:
- **Variable reward** — coin amounts vary slightly, creating anticipation
- **Loss aversion** — streak loss hurts more than gain feels good
- **Social proof** — shareable achievement cards drive organic growth
- **Endowment effect** — users value their tier and won't abandon it easily
- **Commitment escalation** — each payment deepens the user's investment in the platform
